# Credit measurements

Balance before/after measurements were not available to the implementation environment. No per-second STT or per-1,000-character TTS price is assumed.

| Probe | Before (INR) | After (INR) | Measured units | Estimated rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| STT | Not provided | Not provided | Not measured | Not measured |
| TTS | Not provided | Not provided | Not measured | Not measured |

Until measured, the configured `CREDIT_BUDGET_INR` is a hard ceiling, not a price model. The ledger reports unknown estimates as unknown rather than inventing a value.
